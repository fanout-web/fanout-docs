# Submission Demo

Record one continuous two-to-three-minute video. Show the browser address bar and do not edit out transaction confirmation.

## Before recording

1. Open [Fanout](https://fanout-labs.vercel.app) in a clean browser window.
2. Set Freighter to Stellar Testnet and unlock the demo account.
3. Confirm the account holds Testnet XLM for fees and the agreement's accepted test asset.
4. Open the [API health endpoint](https://fanout-labs.vercel.app/api/health) in a second tab and confirm `status` is `ok`.
5. Keep the [contract](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/contract/CCAK6YBIECDQ2GFPMYLV3GWQPJN2DVGJGDHKY76ESZHI56DZMELSTPRV) and [documentation](https://fanout-web.github.io/fanout-docs/) tabs ready.

## Recording sequence

1. State the problem: one incoming payment must be distributed to several beneficiaries without a custodial intermediary.
2. Show the agreement and its basis-point allocations in Fanout.
3. Connect Freighter and confirm that it is on Testnet.
4. Open the payment page, enter a small amount, and submit it.
5. Show the Freighter authorization prompt, including the network and contract invocation.
6. Wait for RPC confirmation. Open the resulting transaction in Stellar Expert.
7. Return to Fanout and show the indexed payment and updated analytics.
8. Show the contract reference and contributor setup in the documentation.
